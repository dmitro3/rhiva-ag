# Get Started 

This guide walks you through your first experience with Rhiva, from connecting your wallet to earning your first liquidity rewards.
Whether you're completely new to DeFi or an experienced liquidity provider, this step-by-step process will help you understand 
how to use the platform effectively.

## Access Rhiva 
Your journey begins by opening the Rhiva web application. Simply navigate to the platform URL in your browser.

You can connect your wallet immediately or explore the interface first. Rhiva supports all major Solana wallets, including:
- Phantom
- Solflare
- Backpack
- And other solana compatable wallets.

No installation or downloads are required beyond your chosen wallet extension.
