# Autoclaim & Autocompounding

Autoclaim and autocompounding are features designed to **automate reward management** and maximize your liquidity efficiency. By enabling these features, you can automatically claim earned rewards and reinvest them without manual intervention.

## Autoclaim
Autoclaim automatically collects your earned rewards from supported pools at set intervals.  

**Benefits:**
- Reduces manual effort
- Ensures rewards are claimed promptly
- Works seamlessly with both DLMM and CLMM pools

**Supported Strategies:**
- Rebalancing pools (e.g., Meteora)

> Autoclaim can be enabled or disabled in your Settings. By default, it is turned off.

## Autocompounding
Autocompounding takes the claimed rewards and **reinvests them directly into the pool**, increasing your liquidity and potential returns over time.  

- Works with Autoclaim
- Helps grow your position automatically
- Best used when concentrating liquidity in tight ranges or with larger capital

> We recommend enabling autocompounding for optimized yield management, especially for active liquidity providers.
