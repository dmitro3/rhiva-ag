# Closing Position

Closing a position allows you to **exit your current liquidity or token position** and realize your gains or losses. This action is essential when your position is out of range, you want to take profits, or you need to reallocate liquidity.

## How to Close a Position
1. Navigate to your **Portfolio** and then **Positions** tab.
2. Click the menu button of the position you want to close.
3. Click the **Close Position** button.   

External wallets only, 

4. Confirm the transaction.

## Notes
- Closing a position will return the underlying tokens to your wallet.
- Any unclaimed rewards associated with the position will be claim automatically.

> Always review your position details before closing to ensure optimal outcomes.
