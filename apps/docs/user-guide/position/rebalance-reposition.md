# Rebalance & Reposition

## Rebalancing
Rebalancing is supported by some DLMM pools, e.g., Meteora pools.  
It redistributes your liquidity across currently active bins.  

This strategy is used by:
- Meteora

## Reposition
Repositioning closes your current out-of-range position and opens a new position with currently active ticks.  
This approach can be more expensive and error-prone due to the liquidity ratio nature of CLMM pools.  

This strategy is used by:
- Orca positions
- Raydium positions
- Saros positions
