## Points Allocation
We reward you with points based on the following criteria:
- 7-day streak: `50 XP`
- 1-month streak: `100 XP`
- Swap volume and open position volume: `1 XP per dollar`
- Referral: `5% of referee points`

> We may forfeit points for anyone caught cheating or manipulating our point allocation system, with sufficient proof and prior warning on their account.

## Tiers
We reward you with NFT badges when you reach certain tiers. Each NFT has a star rating assigned to it.
